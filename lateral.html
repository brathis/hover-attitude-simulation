At datum: tail rotor thrust requires lateral cyclic and fuselage roll to balance its force and moment.
The lateral model sums gravity, main rotor thrust, and tail rotor thrust. Main rotor thrust is adjusted to keep vertical force balanced; tail rotor thrust is fixed at 20% of hover weight to make the one-skid-low attitude easier to see. Dynamics run at 10% acceleration so responses are easier to follow; this does not change force or moment equilibrium. Height is fixed.
